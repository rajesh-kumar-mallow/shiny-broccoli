# Code of Conduct

This is a small, for-fun project that makes Hubble a bit nicer to look at and use. The bar is simple:

- Be kind and respectful in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language/jokes.
- Assume good intent — most disagreements here are about CSS colors, not anything worth being harsh over.
- Feedback on code/UI is welcome and expected; keep it about the work, not the person.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove issues/PRs/comments that don't fit this, and may block repeat offenders. There's no formal enforcement ladder here — this is a hobby repo, not a company policy — but this behavior is expected from everyone participating.

## Reporting

If something crosses the line, open a [GitHub issue](../../issues) describing what happened. If it's sensitive and you'd rather not post publicly, use a private [security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) instead — maintainers will follow up.
